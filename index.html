This Privacy Policy describes how Bajaj Threads collects, uses, protects, and discloses personal and business information when you use our mobile application (Bajaj Thread App) and related commercial order management services.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the business data necessary for B2B wholesale thread ordering and client account management:

  • Personal & Business Identification: Name, phone number, email address, shop name, trade name, GSTIN (optional), PAN (optional), and voluntary identity credentials.
  • Delivery & Logistics Data: Shipping address, city, state, pin code, and preferred transport/courier.
  • Order & Transaction Records: Selected thread varieties, shade numbers, quantities (boxes/dozens), order notes, and order logs.
  • Authentication Data: Secure authentication tokens managed via Google Firebase.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Processing, packing, dispatching, and tracking wholesale orders.
  • Generating official commercial order slips and WhatsApp receipts.
  • Communicating order status updates with clients.
  • Strictly NO selling or renting data to third-party advertisers.

3. Third-Party Services & Data Security

All sensitive data is encrypted using Google Firebase Cloud infrastructure. We do NOT share or sell your data to any data brokers or advertising agencies.

4. Account & Data Deletion

Users may request account deactivation directly within the mobile application under Profile → App Settings & Policies → Deactivate Account, or by emailing our team at support@bajajthreads.com.
